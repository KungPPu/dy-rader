GTA 6's story runs from a prologue through five chapters, with around 91 main missions. Most estimates put the main story at 50 to 65 hours and the full experience with side content at around 80 hours. This guide answers the questions players search most while playing: how many chapters there are, how long it takes, when money gets good, how saving works, and what you can miss. There are no ending spoilers.

Quick answer: GTA 6 has five chapters. Chapters 1 and 2 are the setup, and money opens up from Chapter 3 with jewelry stores and Randy's supercars, then the large bank vaults at the end of Chapter 4. There's no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter.

How many chapters are in GTA 6?

Five. Each chapter changes the setting and the balance between Jason and Lucia, and Chapters 1 and 2 end with a point of no return that takes away your home.

How should you save in GTA 6?

There is no mission replay, so manual saves are the only way to go back.

  • Save in a new slot at the start of each chapter and right before each chapter's final mission.
  • Tourist Trap (Chapter 1) and Checking Out (Chapter 2) are points of no return that remove that chapter's home.
  • The Chapter 3 apartment also closes when the chapter ends, so take your stored items first.
  • Keep a separate save right before the final mission. It's the only way to replay the ending.

What happens to your money and guns when you die?

  • Cash on you is lost. Deposit it at an ATM or bank into BuckMe (the app's ATM Finder points you to the nearest one).
  • BuckMe balance is safe, though a small fee is reported on death.
  • Family Savings can't be withdrawn but earns interest and perks, and is reported to affect the ending. It's also the only money that survives the end of Chapter 1.
  • Weapons bought at a gun store are saved as owned and can be pulled from your trunk or a weapon locker. Picked-up guns are gone once dropped.

GTA 6 chapters FAQ

How many chapters does GTA 6 have?
Five, after a prologue. Chapter 5 is short at seven main missions.

How do I check which chapter I'm on?
The mission list in the pause menu groups missions by chapter, and a title card appears whenever a new chapter starts.

When does money get good?
From Chapter 3, with jewelry stores and Randy's supercars, then the large bank vaults at the end of Chapter 4.

Does the game end after the credits?
No. You return to free roam with your remaining missions, robberies and collectibles.

The short version

GTA 6 has five chapters and takes roughly 50 to 65 hours for the main story. Watch the points of no return in Chapters 1 and 2, build real money from Chapter 3 on, and keep a manual save in a new slot at every chapter break.
