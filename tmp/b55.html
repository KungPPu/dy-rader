Chapter 5 of GTA 6, "Top of the World, Ma," is the final chapter, and it's short: only seven main missions. The last mission is The End of the Road, right after Protective Custody. Because the ending hangs on it, there are a few things worth doing before you go in. This guide covers a spoiler-free checklist for the final mission, how Family Savings relates to the ending, and what's left after the credits.

Quick answer: Before the final mission, the game asks whether you're ready to go ahead; say no and you return to free roam. Make a separate manual save, top up Family Savings (many guides say it affects the ending), fence any loot, and check for open missions. After the credits you can keep playing, and the 100% checklist appears then.

What to do before the final mission

Before The End of the Road starts, the game checks whether you want to go ahead. Choose not yet and you're back in Vice City to finish what you were doing.

  1. Make a separate manual save. Keep the moment right before the final mission in its own slot. It's how you replay the ending later.
  2. Top up Family Savings. Move spare money into it through BuckMe (see below).
  3. Fence your loot. Sell jewelry and marked bills sitting in your trunk.
  4. Check open missions. Look at the mission list for anything still active.
  5. Ultimate Edition reward. If you own it, the '95 Grotti Cheetah unlocks in Chapter 5 at the Pegassi Residences.

Does Family Savings change the ending?

Many guides say the amount in Family Savings shapes how the story ends for Jason and Lucia. They don't agree on exact thresholds, so the safe approach is to put in as much as you reasonably can.

  • Money in Family Savings can't be withdrawn, but there's not much left to buy at this point anyway.
  • Goals are set in tiers, with the final goal documented at $2 million.
  • Short on cash? Rob a few more of the Chapter 4 vaults ($65,000 to $205,000 each) before you go in.
  • The Criminal Profile system comes up in ending discussions too, but it hasn't been confirmed as an ending condition.

Should you finish side content before the ending?

You don't have to. Since the game returns you to free roam after the credits, most side content can wait. There are still a few reasons to do some of it first:

  • Money for Family Savings. Vaults, jewelry stores and Randy's Wish List are the fastest way to grow the balance before the final mission, and that balance is reported to matter.
  • Story context. Some Strangers and Freaks questlines tie into characters you meet in the main story, and they can feel more natural before the ending than after it.
  • A cleaner checklist. Every location you clear now already counts toward 100%, so the post-credits list is shorter.

If you just want to see how it ends, though, make the separate save and go. Nothing in Chapter 5 locks you out of side content.

If you want a different ending

There's no mission replay, so the manual save before the final mission is your only way back. Load it, add more to Family Savings, and try again. If you relied on autosaves, you'll have to go back to an older chapter save.

GTA 6 Chapter 5 FAQ

What is the final mission in GTA 6?
The End of the Road in Chapter 5. The mission before it is Protective Custody.

Does the game end after the credits?
No. You return to free roam with your remaining missions, robberies and collectibles.

Is anything missable in Chapter 5?
Nothing Chapter 5-specific has been identified. Just keep a separate save before the final mission.

How many endings are there?
The ending is reported to vary, with Family Savings as a factor. We don't cover the outcomes here to avoid spoilers.

The short version

Chapter 5 is short, and the two things that matter most are a separate save before the final mission and a healthy Family Savings balance. Vice City stays open after the credits, so there's no need to rush.
