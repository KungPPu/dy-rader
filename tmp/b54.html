The biggest money in GTA 6 comes from the large bank vaults that open at the end of Chapter 4, with a single vault holding $65,000 to $205,000. Chapter 4, "Vice City, Baby," is also the longest chapter at 25 main missions, and it adds a well-paid flying side job along the way. This guide covers how to unlock and rob the vaults, Pilot Pete's deliveries, the easy-to-miss missions, and how to handle Family Savings. Story details are kept to a minimum.

Quick answer: Finish the optional mission "That's Where The Money Is… (Wait at the Cafe)" near the end of Chapter 4 to unlock the eight large bank vaults. A vault is only open while a security van is delivering cash, so follow a van to a bank or wait outside one. Expect an instant 4-star wanted level and plan your getaway first.

When do bank vaults unlock in GTA 6?

Near the very end of Chapter 4, through the optional mission That's Where The Money Is… (Wait at the Cafe). It walks you through your first vault, and after that the eight large banks become repeatable targets. It's worth doing if you're going for 100%.

Robbing five vaults also earns the Maximum Withdrawal trophy or achievement.

How to rob a bank vault, step by step

You can't crack a vault whenever you like. The door only stands open while a security van is dropping off cash.

  1. Find a van. Tail a security van until it stops at a bank, or park outside a branch and wait.
  2. Buy a tip (optional). After you rob two payday loans for Yasmani, he sells security van tips for $10,000. It saves time but costs money.
  3. Follow the guard in. When the guard walks the briefcase into the vault, the door stays open behind them.
  4. Loot everything. To mark the bank complete, take the cash drawer and the safe as well as the vault.
  5. Get out fast. A vault job triggers a 4-star wanted level immediately, so park your getaway car close and know where you'll change clothes.

Vault cash is marked bills, so fence it with Yasmani before you can spend it, and don't die carrying it.

How do Pilot Pete's deliveries work?

Early in Chapter 4, after the main mission Pool Day, Pilot Pete calls Jason. The chain is Jason-only, and every mission in it counts toward 100%.

1The Last Mile: Passing the TorchSundown Private Airfield, Leonard County; pays $12,000

3 and 4Pete's Deliveries: Southpoint Airfield / Avery Allen & Co.Marked together; do either first

  • The delivery runs ask you to drop all seven packages before an in-game deadline (20:00, 14:00 or 08:00).
  • Each successful run is reported to pay $20,000. Check the in-game clock before you take off.

What's easy to miss in Chapter 4?

  • This Is a Free Call From… is a time-limited optional mission for Lucia. Its exact cutoff isn't established, so do it as soon as it appears. It's reported as not needed for 100%.
  • Prep before VCIA Takedown. Guides warn against rushing the chapter's big airport job before finishing the available prep missions. It pays a large story reward, reported at around $250,000.
  • Ultimate Edition boat. Reports conflict on whether the Washington Beach boat is Chapter 4-only, so grab it during Chapter 4 if you own that edition.
  • Manual saves. There's no mission replay, so save in a new slot before each big job.

How much should you put in Family Savings?

Family Savings is the joint BuckMe account Jason and Lucia both pay into. It runs through the whole story as the mission Save for the Future.

  • Money that goes in can't come out. In return, each goal you reach unlocks interest and perks, such as ride discounts at $10,000 and garage perks at $100,000.
  • The final goal is documented at $2 million.
  • Many guides say your Family Savings balance affects the ending. Since vault money starts flowing in Chapter 4, it's a good time to start topping it up.
  • Keep spending money (clothes, guns, cars) in checking and move only the surplus into savings.

GTA 6 Chapter 4 FAQ

Why won't the vault open?
It only opens during a security van delivery. You also need to finish That's Where The Money Is… first.

Why do so many cops show up after a vault job?
Vault robberies trigger a 4-star wanted level instantly. Park your getaway car near the entrance.

Pilot Pete's missions aren't showing up. Why?
The chain starts with a call after Pool Day, and later jobs arrive by text after some in-game time passes. Make sure you're playing as Jason.

Can you withdraw money from Family Savings?
No. It can't be withdrawn or spent, which is the trade-off for the perks.

The short version

Use Pilot Pete's deliveries for mid-chapter cash, then unlock the vaults with the optional mission at the end of Chapter 4. Follow a security van in, plan for four stars, and start building Family Savings once the big money arrives.
