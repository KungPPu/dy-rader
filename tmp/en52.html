Chapter 2 of GTA 6 drops Lucia in Port Gellhorn with almost nothing in her pocket, and the story starts asking for money soon after. The good news is that this is the chapter where a steady, repeatable income finally opens up. This guide covers how to sell stolen cars to Moses, the one car you shouldn't sell, other Chapter 2 money options, how weapon storage works, and what to wrap up before Chapter 3.

Quick answer: After the main mission "Moses (Take a Vehicle to Moses)," you can sell stolen cars without trackers to Moses for roughly $1,000 to $2,000 each. Scan cars with the WAiNK app first, open them quietly with the Slim Jim, and pick Sell, not Register. Keep the first car he registers for free, and deposit your cash at ATMs, because cash on you is lost when you die.

Why is money so tight in Chapter 2?

Everything you earned in Chapter 1 is gone except Family Savings, so Lucia arrives in Port Gellhorn with a few hundred dollars at most.

The Starlet Motel wants rent up front (reported at about $900). If you can't pay, Lucia leaves her necklace as collateral and the debt stays on your story list. Later in the chapter, the story also sets a cash goal you need before moving to Vice City (reported at about $15,000). That's why the first priority is unlocking one reliable way to earn, and that's Moses.

How do you sell cars to Moses in GTA 6?

Moses is a mechanic your friend Vicki points you to. Finish the main mission Moses (Take a Vehicle to Moses) and he becomes a buyer for stolen cars. The same mission gives you the Slim Jim, which opens standard locks without setting off the alarm.

  1. Scan the car. Open the WAiNK app on your phone and hold R1 on a car. It shows the tracker, alarm and sell value. Only take cars where the tracker reads None. Moses refuses Standard or Advanced trackers.
  2. Open it quietly. At the door, press Triangle and choose the Slim Jim instead of smashing the window, then find the sweet spot with the stick.
  3. Pick your spot. Cars parked alone at the back of a lot are safest. If someone sees you take a car off a busy street, they call the cops.
  4. Check the price. While sitting in the car, hover over Moses on the map to see that car's sell value and registration fee.
  5. Sell it. Park inside the garage and choose Sell. Register means paying to keep the car, so don't mix them up.

Don't sell your first registered car

Moses registers the first car you bring him for free. After that, registration gets expensive, often well over $3,000, which is a lot of Chapter 2 money.

  • His menu also lets you sell the car you're driving, and a sale can't be undone. Keep the free one.
  • If your first car has an alarm, he sends you back for a better one, so bring something you actually want to keep.
  • The motel lot only holds cars temporarily. For long-term parking, use the garage at the Port Gellhorn station.
  • If your registered car is across the map, the Scooter Bros app delivers it for around $100. A one-time premium upgrade gives you three free deliveries a day.

Where do you store weapons in GTA 6?

You carry two handguns and two long guns. Everything else lives in your car trunk or a weapon locker.

  • Trunk: at the back of your car, "manage loadout" swaps what you carry and "open storage" moves guns and items in and out. Space depends on the vehicle.
  • Gellhorn Outfitters: besides selling guns, it lets you take weapons you already own out of the locker, so check before you buy the same gun twice.
  • Attachments and what you equip are set per character.

How do you lose the cops faster?

While police are searching, open the map to see what they know: vehicle, appearance or outfit. Change only what they've spotted.

  • Outfit: put on a new top. Spare outfits stored in your wardrobe can be swapped from the trunk.
  • Appearance: change your hairstyle or hair color at a salon.
  • Vehicle: respray it at Pay 'n' Spray. You can't drive in with cops nearby, and a damaged car needs repairs first.

What to do before Chapter 3

Chapter 2 ends with the move to Vice City, and there's no going back once it starts.

  1. Empty the motel. The Starlet Motel stops being your hideout once the final mission begins, so take anything stored there.
  2. Make a manual save. There's no mission replay. Save in a new slot at the start of the chapter and right before the last mission.
  3. Bank your cash. The game itself tells you to put the Vice City goal money into BuckMe. Filling the first $5,000 Family Savings goal also earns interest.
  4. Finish local side activities. Small Port Gellhorn extras, like the pool game at the Delights club, are reported to be gone after the move. Jason's hunting jobs and Moses's vehicle jobs remain available later.

GTA 6 Chapter 2 FAQ

Why won't Moses take my car?
Check the tracker in WAiNK. He refuses tracked cars, cars stolen near his garage, and anything you bring right after a sale.

Where do you sell cars with trackers?
From Chapter 3. Pay 'n' Spray buys cars with standard trackers, reportedly for around $4,000 each.

Do you lose your money at the end of Chapter 2?
There's no sign of a Chapter 1-style reset. The game actually tells you to keep the Vice City goal money in BuckMe.

Where do you store clothes and weapons?
Clothes go in your hideout wardrobe, and guns bought at Ammu-Nation are saved as owned weapons. Your car trunk lets you change outfits and swap guns on the go.

The short version

Unlock Moses early, scan every car with WAiNK, sell only untracked ones, and bank your cash at every ATM. Keep the free registered car, and clear out the motel before the final mission.
