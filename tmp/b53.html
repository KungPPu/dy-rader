Chapter 3 of GTA 6, "Tits, Ass, Drug Money," is where the money finally opens up. In Chapter 2 a good car sale paid a couple of thousand dollars; in Chapter 3 a single jewelry store can pay $25,000 and one supercar for Randy pays $30,000 or more. This guide covers what unlocks when, which fence to sell to, tips for the Cartel Heist, and what to wrap up before Chapter 4. Story details are kept to a minimum.

Quick answer: Chapter 3 unlocks money in this order: Pay 'n' Spray buys cars with standard trackers, Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs open jewelry store robberies, Credit Check opens small banks and payday loans, and the Key Cloner opens Randy's Wish List of 18 supercars. Sell jewelry and marked bills to Yasmani and narcotics to Tyrone, and empty your Vice City apartment before the chapter ends.

Selling tracked cars at Pay 'n' Spray

In Chapter 2, Moses only took cars without trackers. From Chapter 3, Pay 'n' Spray also buys cars with a Standard tracker.

  • Scan with the WAiNK app first to see the tracker type and sell value.
  • Police know where a tracked car is from the moment you take it, so pick cars close to a Pay 'n' Spray.
  • There's a short cooldown between sales.

How do you rob jewelry stores in GTA 6?

The main mission Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs opens jewelry store robberies and gives you the Snap Gun for fast lockpicking. Smaller shops are reported at $1,000 to $10,000 and the top jewelers at $14,000 to $25,000.

  1. Case the store. Walk in unarmed and note staff, cameras and the back room.
  2. Kill the security first. Most guides say to shut down the security system in the back room before anything else, or the heat builds fast.
  3. Hit the cases and the safe. Look around for a note with the safe combination.
  4. Fence it. Stolen jewelry isn't money until a fence buys it.

Randy's supercars are the best Chapter 3 money

Halfway through the chapter, the mission Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS gives you the Key Cloner, which opens cars with advanced trackers, and introduces Randy. You deliver a Zorrusso to him, and bringing it in undamaged is reported to add a bonus of about $10,000.

  • Randy hands you a Wish List of 18 high-end cars. Each pays $30,000 or more.
  • Damage cuts the price, so open cars quietly and avoid ramming your way out of a chase.
  • He only takes cars on the list, so check the model in WAiNK first.

Small banks pay in marked bills

After Credit Check you can rob 14 small banks and 7 payday loan stores. A bank only shows as complete on the map once you've looted both the cash drawer and the safe.

The cash comes out as marked bills, which you can't spend until a fence like Yasmani buys them. Marked bills on you are lost if you die, so fence them right away. The big vault robberies come later, at the end of Chapter 4.

Tips before the Cartel Heist

Chapter 3 ends with The Cartel Heist, and the missions before it collect the gear, vehicles and crew you need.

  • Plant all three bombs in Hidden Charges. Doing so is reported to give you more time inside the vault during the heist.
  • Save before choices. Chapter 3 has decisions, such as selling product pure or cut in Dealer's Choice. There's no mission replay, so make a manual save first.
  • Timed side mission. Jason's optional Race Against Yago is time-limited. Do it as soon as its hourglass marker appears. It isn't needed for 100%.
  • Finishing the chapter pays a large story reward, reported at about $120,000.

What to do before Chapter 4

  1. Empty the apartment. The Vice City apartment you get in Welcome to Vice City closes after Chapter 3. Take your stored items, and grab the two magazines inside (copies exist elsewhere too).
  2. Sell your loot. Fence everything in your trunk and bank the cash in BuckMe.
  3. Park your cars safely. Keep the cars you care about in a hideout garage.
  4. Make a manual save in a new slot right before The Cartel Heist.

GTA 6 Chapter 3 FAQ

I stole jewelry but got no money. Why?
Jewelry is loot. You have to sell it to a fence, and Yasmani in Little Cuba pays the most.

Why won't Randy take my car?
Make sure it's on his Wish List and that it isn't badly damaged. Advanced-tracker cars need the Key Cloner.

Why can't I spend the money from a bank?
Bank cash is marked bills. Sell them to a fence before you can use them.

Is anything missable in Chapter 3?
Items left in the apartment and the two magazines inside it. The timed side mission doesn't count toward 100%.

The short version

Build a bankroll with jewelry stores, then switch to Randy's supercars once you have the Key Cloner. Sell jewelry and marked bills to Yasmani, and clear out the apartment before the Cartel Heist ends the chapter.
